Chapter 1 got a standalone appliance running and authenticated. This chapter is about keeping it in shape: who can reach its shell, which certificates it presents and signs with, and how to make it survive the loss of a node. The second half covers the things you set once and rely on for years: where its logs go, how to get it back after a bad day, how much memory it gets, and the system properties that change its defaults.

Everything here applies to the **standalone appliance** only. The Orchestrator embedded in VCF Automation is managed as part of VCF Automation, and you don't get a root shell on it.

## Where the settings went

Almost every recipe in this chapter starts at the appliance's root shell. That's the biggest change for anyone who ran vRO 7 or early vRO 8.

## A word on restarts

Several recipes end with `/opt/scripts/deploy.sh`. That script stops and redeploys every service on the appliance, so Orchestrator is unavailable while it runs, and running workflows are interrupted. Plan certificate, cluster, and memory changes for a maintenance window, and take a VM snapshot first (recipe 2.6).
