This is a free cookbook for **VCF Operations Orchestrator 9.x**, the workflow engine at the centre of VMware Cloud Foundation automation. It's for people who are new to Orchestrator, and for people who learned it years ago as vCO or vRO and want to know what has changed.

## Who writes it

The book is written by **Mayank** ([@imtrinity94](https://github.com/imtrinity94), author of [cloudblogger.co.in](https://cloudblogger.co.in)) and **Claude**, an AI model made by Anthropic. Claude researches each topic against current Broadcom documentation and drafts the recipes. Mayank reviews every page, checks it against a VCF 9.x lab, and takes the screenshots.

## How it's written

The structure follows the recipe format that made the *VMware vRealize Orchestrator Cookbook* (Daniel Langenhan, Packt, 2015) so useful: each recipe is one task with **Getting ready**, **How to do it**, **How it works**, **There's more**, and **See also**. That book covered vRO 5.5 and 6. This one is written from scratch for Orchestrator 9.x. No text, examples, or images from the original are reused.

While a chapter is in draft, you'll see two kinds of marker:

- **Screenshot to capture** marks a figure that is planned but not yet taken in the lab.
- **Needs a lab check** marks a statement that comes from documentation but hasn't been confirmed on a running system yet.

## Versions

Unless a recipe says otherwise, it was written against **VCF 9.1** with Orchestrator **9.1.1**. Most recipes also apply to 9.0.
