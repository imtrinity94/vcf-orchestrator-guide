## Where to start

- **New to Orchestrator?** Read chapter 1 in order, then chapter 3 (visual programming), then chapter 4 (scripting).
- **Coming from vRO 7 or earlier?** Start with the introduction to chapter 1. It lists what's gone and what replaced it.
- **Using Orchestrator through VCF Automation?** Read recipe 1.1, then jump to chapter 8.

## What you need

A VCF 9.x environment with either the Orchestrator embedded in VCF Automation, or a standalone Orchestrator appliance. Chapter 1 covers both. For the plug-in and infrastructure chapters, you'll also need a vCenter you're allowed to experiment on.
